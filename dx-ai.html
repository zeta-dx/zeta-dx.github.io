About Dx AI

Dx AI is an educational laboratory report analysis tool developed by Zeta Dx to help users better understand the information contained in their laboratory reports.

The tool is designed to identify results that are outside the reference intervals provided by the laboratory. Where sufficient information is available, Dx AI may also perform relevant calculations, unit conversions and analysis of relationships between related laboratory parameters.

Dx AI may provide explanations of laboratory parameters, possible factors associated with abnormal results and information about tests that may be relevant for further discussion with a qualified healthcare professional.

The reference ranges printed on the uploaded laboratory report will be used as the primary reference for identifying results outside the laboratory's stated interval.

What Dx AI Can Do

  • Identify laboratory results that are outside the reference interval supplied by the laboratory.
  • Explain what individual laboratory parameters represent.
  • Convert laboratory values between commonly used units where applicable.
  • Perform relevant calculations when the required information is available.
  • Examine relationships between related laboratory parameters.
  • Provide educational information about possible explanations for abnormal findings.
  • Highlight information that may be useful to discuss with a healthcare professional.

⚠ Important Warning

Dx AI is intended for educational and informational purposes only.

Dx AI does not provide a medical diagnosis and should not be used as a substitute for examination, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.

Laboratory results can be affected by many factors, including age, sex, medical history, medications, fasting status, sample collection conditions, laboratory methods and other clinical circumstances.

Do not start, stop or change any medication, supplement or treatment based solely on information provided by this tool.

If you have concerning symptoms or have received a result that may require urgent medical attention, seek appropriate medical care.

How the Analysis Will Work

Dx AI is being designed to preserve the information reported by the laboratory rather than replacing laboratory-specific reference intervals with a generic reference range.

Reported values will be separated from values calculated or converted by Zeta Dx. For example, an eGFR, ACR, eAG or converted unit may be presented as a calculated value rather than as a result directly reported by the laboratory.

Where required information is unavailable, Dx AI will indicate that a calculation or interpretation cannot be reliably performed instead of inventing missing information.
